Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Nota is a static web app with no backend server and no database of its own. It stores your financial data only in a Google Sheet that it creates in your own Google Drive, using your Google account's own storage.

What we access

When you sign in with Google, Nota requests the drive.file permission scope. This scope only grants access to files that Nota itself creates — it cannot see, read, or modify any of your other Google Drive files or Sheets.

Optional: Email Import (Gmail, read-only)

Email Import is off by default. Only if you switch it on (Profile › Email Import), Nota additionally asks for the read-only gmail.readonly scope. Nota then searches your Gmail only for emails from the senders you list under Email Import (it starts with a few bank notification senders, and you can change, remove or add your own, such as receipts), reads those messages inside your browser, and shows them as suggested transactions that you confirm one by one. Email content is processed on your device only and is never sent to any server run by Nota or to any third party. Only the transactions you confirm, plus your matching rules and the IDs of already-handled emails, are saved to your own Google Sheet. Nota never sends, deletes or modifies email. Turning the switch off stops Nota from requesting Gmail access; you can also remove it entirely from your Google Account permissions page. Nota's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we store

Nota has no server, so it cannot and does not store your data anywhere outside your own Google account. Your browser keeps a small amount of local data (via localStorage) — the ID of the Sheet it created for you — purely so it can find your data again on your next visit.

What we don't do

  • We do not have access to your other Drive files or contacts, and we do not read your email unless you opt in to Email Import above.
  • We do not run any analytics or tracking scripts.
  • We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party.

Revoking access

You can revoke Nota's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. The Sheet Nota created remains in your Drive — you can keep, export, or delete it yourself.
